- Search snippet: Planterra "no design fee", rental from $200/month; Alphaplantes "no initial payment". URLs: https://planterra.com/services/office-plants/ , https://alphaplantes.com/en/pages/rental (2026)

**Pattern summary (packaging):**
1. B2B: tiered bundles by office size (m² or headcount) with a monthly floor of €200/AED ~750 seem to be the modern-startup pattern (Plantclub, WELO); legacy firms price per plant (€6–15/mo) with a €75–100 monthly minimum and 12–24 month terms.
2. Nobody uses "free first month". The common concessions are: free design concept/visualisation, free site visit, free delivery/installation, free replacement guarantee. Setup fee patterns: none (Plantclub/WELO), one month's rent (Plantman), $50 + first & last month (Greenleaf).
3. Minimum term: 12 months is the norm; Plantman month-to-month; Reiffer 24 months; Plantclub once had 3 months.
4. Visit cadence: every 2 weeks (Plantclub, Plantman, Berlin care-only), every 3 weeks (WELO, Reiffer), every 4 weeks (Gregg); Dubai providers advertise **weekly** (800petals, Plantshop.me, Royal Plantscape weekly tier) — hot/dry climate + AC drives more frequent watering.

## 5. Synthesis for Planty

### 5.1 Market choice (GTM view only)
- Berlin: transparent, crowded B2B SERP; a well-funded direct analogue (Plantclub, 150+ Berlin teams) plus 6+ legacy Hydrokultur firms; cheap clicks (€1.5–3); B2C rental essentially non-existent; price floor €200/mo net for a small office.
- Dubai: opaque quote-only competitors, no public rental price list, WhatsApp-driven sales, weekly-visit expectation, visible B2C home-plant-care demand (AED 99–499 price points), summer-absence pain, expat/relocation and holiday-home operator channels, but higher CPCs (AED 12–25 ≈ €3–6) and B2B CPL AED 150–900.
- GTM verdict: Dubai offers a clearer positioning wedge ("transparent, instant online pricing + weekly care + seasonal rotation") and a real B2C segment; Berlin is a "beat Plantclub on price/flexibility" fight. Combined with the founder's Dubai product history, Dubai is the recommended single-city launch from a GTM lens, with Berlin as the second city once ops are proven.

### 5.2 Pricing/packaging recommendation (estimate, anchored on sources above)
- Publish prices online (contrarian in Dubai). Per-plant list price AED 60–150/mo by size, with bundles:
  - Home Starter (apartment): 4 plants, visit every 2 weeks, ~AED 349–449/mo
  - Home Plus: 8 plants, weekly, ~AED 699–899/mo
  - Villa: 15+ plants incl. large statement plants, weekly, from ~AED 1,500/mo + styling add-on
  - Office S / M / L by m² or desks, weekly, from ~AED 999/mo (S ≈ 10 plants), matching the €200-floor logic of Plantclub/WELO scaled to Dubai maintenance prices.
- Term: B2B 12 months (industry norm; cash-flow); B2C 3-month minimum then monthly (Plantclub's old 3-month model shows it is workable). Offer seasonal swap included quarterly; no "free month"; instead free design mock-up + free install (matches all competitors) and a refundable deposit only for B2C short terms (Greenleaf "first and last month" pattern).
- Cadence: weekly in Dubai (all local competitors), bi-weekly in Berlin.

### 5.3 First-10-offices concierge playbook
1. Build a list of 100 target offices (30–150 m²) in DIFC/Business Bay/Media City/JLT (800petals' own named districts) + all 129 Dubai coworking spaces and 4 Servcorp sites.
2. Founder-led outreach (LinkedIn + WhatsApp + walk-ins), offering a free site visit and a visual concept within 48 h (copy Plantclub), quote within 24 h (copy 800petals).
3. Pilot offer: 3-month pilot at list price, no setup fee, cancel anytime after month 3, upgrade to 12-month gets 1 month free (only concession).
4. Install within 14 days; weekly visit; photo report after each visit (differentiator, cheap to build in the MVP).
5. Ask every pilot for a LinkedIn post + referral (referral leads ~$14 vs $45–150 paid; target 20% of leads from referral).

### 5.4 90-day plan (single city, Dubai)
- Days 0–30: pricing page + WhatsApp CTA live (Next.js); nursery/supplier + pot SKUs; 20 site visits booked via founder outreach; Google Search campaign AED 5k/mo on "office plant rental dubai", "indoor plant rental dubai", "plant maintenance dubai"; Instagram account with install reels.
- Days 31–60: first 5 offices + 10 homes installed; weekly route running; collect photo/testimonial content; approach 3 relocation firms + 3 holiday-home operators + 2 FM firms with a partner rate; test B2C "Summer plant-sitting → convert to rental" offer.
- Days 61–90: 10 offices / 25 homes target; measure CAC per channel against the AED 2–4k (B2B) / AED 250–500 (B2C) estimates; decide on 12-month push vs flexible term based on churn at month 3; prepare Berlin thesis if Dubai unit economics hold.
