Takeaways (DE): the published market clears at roughly **€7–14 per plant per month** for standard office plants, with **3–4-weekly** care visits (hydroculture / self-watering systems make this possible), **12–24-month minimum terms**, and **minimum invoices of €75–199/month**. ### 2e. Technician productivity, visit time, route density
- Newpro Containers pricing guide: maintenance labour = visit hours × hourly rate × visits/month; worked example **"0.75 hours × $30/hour × 5 visits/month = $112.50 per month"**; replacement allowance = retail plant price × 1.3–1.5 ÷ 12 per month; leasing = retail × ≥1.5 ÷ 12; freight can add "a whopping 30%". Advice: set a minimum dollar threshold per visit "to justify leaving the office". https://www.newprocontainers.com/blog/quick-guide-pricing-interiorscape-jobs/ (undated, US)
- Inside Plants (US) job ad: technicians "hit as few as five or as many as **twenty locations a day**", own vehicle, paid hourly + drive time + mileage, 8:00–4:30. https://insideplants.net/job-openings/ (2025) — ANECDOTAL
- MacBeth Farm Interiorscapes job ad (NJ): 6.5–7.5 h/day, **4–5 accounts some days, up to 10 others**; $17–19/h (2025, search snippet) — ANECDOTAL
- ZipRecruiter: interiorscape jobs **$15–26/h** (Aug 2026 snippet); plantscape $14–24/h (Jul 2026 snippet).
- Büropflanzen Pflege Berlin: "a few minutes per plant" (2025).
- EST used in model: **2.5–3 min per plant + 10–15 min per site** (access, log, bins), plus **20 min drive + parking per stop in Berlin, 15 min in Dubai**. A Berlin tech on bi-weekly office routes does ~6 stops/day; a Dubai tech on weekly routes ~8 stops/day (denser commercial clusters, no parking search, but traffic).

### 2f. Vehicle / delivery cost per stop
- No public per-km figure was retrievable within budget. EST: Berlin small van (leased, insured, fuel, parking) ≈ €0.45/km → 8 km/stop ≈ €3.6 + driver time (in labour) → **€11–12 per stop all-in**. Dubai: fuel ~AED 3/litre (2025, general knowledge), longer distances (~15 km/stop) but cheaper fuel and labour → **AED 12–18 per stop all-in**. WEAKEST INPUT together with wholesale prices.

### 2g. Plant lifespan and replacement rate
- Search-snippet statement (officeplants.com, 2026): "professional services maintain a plant survival rate of **95% or higher**, with free replacements built into the contract".
- Interior Plantscapes blog: some offices rotate plants **every 30 days** for visual consistency; "even the most resilient indoor plants begin to lose visual sharpness after a few weeks in high-traffic office zones". https://interiorplantscapes.com/why-some-offices-secretly-replace-their-plants-every-30-days/ (2025) — extreme end.
- Oakland Green Interiors warranty: replacement free only if decline is from disease, insects or watering when OGI maintains; excluded: employee/visitor damage, HVAC/climate, theft, relocation, insufficient light, construction dust/spills; guarantee suspended when 30+ days overdue. https://oaklandgreeninteriors.com/warranty-service-level-agreement/ (2024)
- hydroflora: 3-month acclimation guarantee on plants (2025).
- Plants Xpert Dubai: "7-day green guarantee" (2025).
- Financial Models Lab (indoor plant retail store model): inventory shrink 10%, product COGS 10–12% of revenue, services ~2% materials (2025) — retail, not rental.
- No published annual replacement percentage for rental fleets was found. EST: **20–25%/yr in German hydroculture/self-watering offices; 30–35%/yr for weekly-watered soil plants in Dubai (AC dryness, 40°C delivery shocks); 50%+/yr for outdoor villa pots in summer**. WEAK.

### Technician-level economics (derived)
- Berlin: 1 tech, 7 productive h/day, bi-weekly office routes of ~6 stops/day × 10 days = 60 accounts × 25 plants = 1,500 plants × €13 = **~€19.5k/month revenue per technician (~€230k/yr)**; tech + van cost ≈ €5.5k/month → route-level margin before plants/overheads ~70%. Requires ~60 signed offices per technician — the density hurdle for a solo founder.
- Dubai: weekly routes of ~8 stops/day × 5 days = 40 accounts × 25 plants = 1,000 plants × AED 60 = **AED 60k/month (~€14.5k) per technician** at a technician cost of ~AED 4.5k/month.
- Contract value: Berlin small office ≈ €225–350/month (25 plants); WELO's package ladder €199/€299/€649 (2025) confirms typical ticket sizes. Dubai ≈ AED 1,000–1,900/month.

## 5. Weakest assumptions (ranked)
1. **Dubai per-plant rental price** — no operator publishes it; AED 60/plant is inferred from maintenance packages and US pricing. Needs 3 mystery-shop quotes (800petals, Plantscapes, Plantsworld).
2. **Wholesale plant costs (both markets)** — inferred from retail; FloraHolland/Landgard and Dubai nursery-gate quotes needed.
3. **Vehicle/travel cost per stop and stops per day** — no local data; Berlin parking and Dubai traffic could each move visit cost ±30%.
4. **Annual replacement rate** — only the "95% survival" claim exists; Dubai heat/AC and B2C misuse could push it well above 30%.
5. **Time per plant** — 2.5 min is consistent with "a few minutes per plant" and Newpro's 45-min visit example but unverified for Planty's plant mix.
