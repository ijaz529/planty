# Planty research — Lens: Market size & growth (raw notes)

Researched 2026-09-09. Every number carries the year it refers to and the URL it came from. "Reported" = taken from the cited source; "Estimate" = my own arithmetic on reported inputs, flagged as such. Web-search budget was exhausted mid-session (~20 distinct queries run); the remaining verification was done by fetching the source pages directly.

---

## 0. Headline picture (TL;DR)

- There is **no credible published market-size figure for "plant rental / interiorscaping" as a stand-alone industry** from the major houses (Grand View, IBISWorld, Statista, Technavio, Mordor). IBISWorld and Statista do not carve it out; the "Plant Hire" reports that show up in search are construction-equipment reports, not living plants. The best anchors are (a) the commercial segment of the indoor-plant market, (b) Ambius (Rentokil Initial) disclosures, and (c) one trade-press estimate (~USD 3.5bn global plant-care/interiorscaping, 2024).
- **Consumer houseplants** are a ~USD 12–22bn global market (2025, range across six research houses) growing ~4–5 %/yr; residential is 60–76 % of it. Germany's Zimmerpflanzen retail market is **€1.4bn (2025), down 4.5 % YoY** after €1.5bn (2024); per-capita spend on green houseplants is under €6/yr (2025).
- **Germany (B2B):** the FvRH/BuGG describe 2024 as "stabil, jedoch spürbar wechselhaft"; Berlin office vacancy hit **10.4 % (end-2025)**, take-up the lowest since 2009, home-office share stable at ~25 % of workers.
- **UAE (B2B):** Dubai office occupancy **92 % citywide, 95 % Grade A (H1 2025)**, prime vacancy **0.3 % (Nov 2025)**, rents +22 % YoY; Dubai population passed **4.0m (Sept 2025)**, +6 % in a year, 92 % expats. UAE landscaping market **USD 1.67bn (2024) → 2.84bn (2030), 9.07 % CAGR** (TechSci), Dubai the largest share.
- **Rental / product-as-a-service:** furniture-rental services grow 8–10 %/yr globally (2025); Germany ~6.6 % CAGR to 2035 (FMI). Plant-subscription boxes are a "multimillion-dollar", ~10 %-CAGR niche with no hard size figure.

## 1. Interior landscaping / plant rental & maintenance ("plantscaping")

### 1.1 What the market-research houses actually cover

- Searches for "plant rental market", "plant hire market", "interior landscaping market" return either (a) **construction plant-hire** reports (e.g. Verified Market Reports: "Plant Hire Market USD 153.5bn in 2024" — equipment, irrelevant; https://www.verifiedmarketreports.com/product/plant-hire-market/ ; Valuates/QYResearch "Plant Hire USD 744m 2023 → 1,156m 2030, 6.6 % CAGR" — definition could not be verified because the domain was blocked; treat as equipment until proven otherwise; https://reports.valuates.com/market-reports/QYRE-Auto-19D6361/global-plant-hire), or (b) **outdoor landscaping services** (global USD 330bn 2024, Grand View: https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/landscaping-services-market-report ; US USD 184–189bn 2025, NALP/IBISWorld via https://www.landscapeprofessionals.org/LP/LP/Media/landscape-industry-statistics.aspx and https://www.youraspire.com/blog/landscaping-industry-statistics).
- IBISWorld has no dedicated "interior plant rental" industry in the US/UK/AU catalogues found; the Australian "Gardening Services" industry lists "indoor plant and shrub services" as a sub-line only (https://www.ibisworld.com/australia/industry/gardening-services/676/). IBISWorld Australia "Nursery Production" revenue fell 8.4 % annualised over five years to AUD 1.08bn (2025-26), while "indoor plants and patio lines have performed more robustly" (https://ibisworld.com/australia/industry/nursery-production/4).
- Conclusion: **any TAM for Planty must be built bottom-up** (offices × penetration × spend) or triangulated from the commercial slice of the indoor-plant market.

### 1.2 Trade-press estimate of the interiorscaping industry

Landscape Management, "How interiorscaping is evolving in the post-pandemic workplace" (published 5 March 2026): https://www.landscapemanagement.net/how-interiorscaping-is-evolving-in-the-post-pandemic-workplace/
- Reported: global plant-care/interiorscaping market "approximately **$3.5 billion in 2024**", growing at a "high single-digit" rate, "could roughly double over the next decade". (Source of the $3.5bn is not named in the article; treat as trade-press estimate.)
- Reported: Ambius **£141.3m ongoing revenue in 2021 (~USD 190m)**; since 2022 Ambius is folded into Rentokil's "Hygiene & Wellbeing" category. Pre-pandemic growth "modest, low single-digit annual growth".
- Quote (Shane Pliska, Planterra): "Interior landscaping is a year-round service... it requires having two types of staff, along with different training, suppliers and equipment."
- Quote (Rebecca Bullene, Greenery NYC): "a desire to see nature thrive over time, not just die a slow death in a dark conference room."

## 4. Drivers

1. **Biophilic design / wellbeing (B2B)** — global biophilic-design market forecast USD 3.14bn by 2028, 10.2 % CAGR 2023–28 (source not verified; from K2 Space/Accio secondary); WELL Building Institute 2022: 84 % believe biophilic design improves wellbeing; CBRE 2021: 75 % of occupants "willing to pay extra for biophilic design features" (https://k2space.co.uk/knowledge/biophilic-office-design). Mordor quantifies corporate WELL/LEED certifications as +0.6 pt of indoor-plant CAGR (2025).
2. **Return-to-office / flight to quality** — Dubai: occupiers "increasingly favouring Grade A, ESG-compliant buildings" (Emirates NBD H1 2025), lease lengths now 7–9 yrs vs 3–5 (JLL Nov 2025). Germany: no RTO trend ("Ein Trend zur Rückkehr ins Büro ist nicht erkennbar", ifo Sept 2025), but landlords of older stock must upgrade amenities to compete with 10 % vacancy — plants are a cheap amenity.
3. **Renters who move often (B2C)** — Dubai: 92 % expats, +6 % population growth (2025), seasonal tenant churn; Berlin: 25 % of German workers use home office (Destatis 2025: https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/04/PD26_N024_13.html — 25 % in 2025, 24 % 2024, 23 % 2023, 13 % 2019), which raises home-environment spend but not office spend.
4. **Low-maintenance preference** — 72 % of buyers prioritise low-maintenance plants (GGI 2025); Mordor: low-light species 46 % share. A zero-maintenance rental directly answers this.
5. **ESG / circularity** — Dubai new supply "largely composed of Grade A, ESG-compliant space" (C&W Q2 2025); German firms cite ESG compliance as a structural driver of office demand (REFIRE 2025).
6. **Hospitality/coworking** — FvRH: trend "gerade im Bereich der Hotels oder bei Coworking-Spaces" (2022); UAE tourism target 40m hotel guests by 2031.

## 5. Headwinds

1. **Consumer houseplant demand is contracting in Germany** — €1.5bn (2024) → €1.4bn (2025), −4.5 %; per-capita green-plant spend <€6/yr (ZVG/AMI 2026). Cost-of-living squeeze named explicitly.
2. **Berlin office market weakness** — vacancy 10.4 %, 2.24m m² empty, take-up 484,200 m² (−11 %, lowest since 2009), average rent −11 % to €25.90/m² (C&W Jan 2026: https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/germany/news/2026/01/office-leasing-berlin); stock 21.7m m², 880k office workers (end-2024) (REFIRE Sept 2025: https://www.refire-online.com/markets/berlin-offices-face-9-percent-vacancy-forecast-despite-employment-growth/). Vacancy expected >11 % in 2026.
3. **German interiorscape sector itself flags rising costs, skilled-labour shortage and construction/real-estate uncertainty** (BuGG/FvRH 2025 report on 2024).
4. **Labour intensity** — year-round service with two staff types (Planterra quote); US price points of $150–2,000/mo per office imply thin margins for a solo founder without route density.
5. **UAE climate/ops** — water scarcity and desalination costs (TechSci 2024); summer heat and import dependence on Dutch nurseries (not quantified here — open question).
6. **Post-pandemic normalisation** — US spend stagnated 2024; DTC plant retailers (The Sill) lost money and closed stores; VC funding for plant startups dried up after 2021.
7. **Rental-vs-buy economics** — German consumer media: "Auf lange Sicht ist Mieten fast immer teurer als Kaufen" (2026).

## 7. Bottom-up TAM sketches (my estimates, not reported figures)

- **Berlin B2B**: 21.7m m² stock × (1 − 10.4 % vacancy) ≈ 19.4m m² occupied. At Plantclub's list price (€200/mo per ≤150 m² → ~€1.3/m²/mo; €600/mo per ≤1,000 m² → €0.6/m²/mo) a fully-penetrated market would be €140–300m/yr; at a realistic 5–10 % penetration of occupied space, **€7–30m/yr serviceable market in Berlin**. Incumbents (Ambius DE, Plantclub 150+ members, Lifestyle Raumbegrünung, Kremkau, etc.) already share this.
- **Berlin B2C**: 3.8m people × <€6/yr green-houseplant spend ≈ €20m/yr on green plants; a €25/mo subscription is 50× the average person's annual spend, so the addressable group is a small design-affine, high-income, high-mobility slice.
- **Dubai B2B**: cannot be sized without office stock; qualitatively, 92–95 % occupancy, rents +22 %, ESG Grade A pipeline and 7–9-year leases are the opposite of Berlin's conditions.
- **Dubai B2C**: 4.0m residents, 92 % expats, ~230k net new residents in 2025; villa segment is a distinct, higher-ticket target.
