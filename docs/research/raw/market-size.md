# Planty — Research Lens: Market Size & Growth

Compiled 2026-09-10 (research date). Every figure below carries its source year and URL.
Labels used: **[MR]** = commercial market-research vendor (treat with caution, methodology usually opaque),
**[ASSOC]** = industry association / national statistics, **[CORP]** = company filings/reports,
**[PRESS]** = journalism, **[ANEC]** = anecdotal (vendor blog, forum, marketing page), **[EST]** = my own derived estimate.

---

## 0. Executive read (before the detail)

1. There is **no clean, credible, global "plant rental" market number**. The category is buried inside
   larger buckets: interior landscaping, facility-management soft services, and "plant care services".
   Anyone quoting a precise global plantscaping TAM is extrapolating. Treat with suspicion.
2. The closest thing to a category-specific figure is Future Market Insights' **"Plant Care Services"
   market: USD 3,806.7m in 2025 → USD 9,620.6m by 2035, 9.7% CAGR**, of which **on-site visits = 65.1%**
   and **commercial offices = 39.8%** of 2025 demand. [MR]
3. The **consumer houseplant market is shrinking in Germany right now** (indoor plants −4.5% in 2025) while
   the **UAE/Gulf indoor and landscaping market is compounding at ~9–10%**. That is the single sharpest
   divergence in this whole lens.
4. The **office-side demand signal is inverted between the two candidate markets**: Berlin office vacancy
   was **10.4% at end-2025 and rising**, with take-up 40% below the 10-year average; **Dubai prime office
   vacancy was 0.3% in late 2025**. Planty's B2B motion is a bet on occupied, growing office floorspace.
5. The B2C "rent a plant" motion has **no market-research validation anywhere** — it is a genuinely
   unproven category. The adjacent proven B2C category is the **plant subscription box** (buy, not rent),
   and its flagship players have been contracting, not growing.

## 1. Interior landscaping / plantscaping / plant rental

### 1.1 The "Plant Care Services" market — the closest category-specific figure

Future Market Insights, *Plant Care Services Market* (accessed 2026-09) [MR]
https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/plant-care-services-market

| Metric | Value | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Market value | USD 3,806.7 million | 2025 |
| Forecast value | USD 9,620.6 million | 2035 |
| CAGR | 9.7% | 2025–2035 |
| On-site visits share of service mode | 65.1% | 2025 |
| Commercial offices share of customer type | 39.8% | 2025 |
| North America share | 37.4% | 2025 |

Regional CAGRs 2025–2035 from the same report: **Germany 10.6%**, USA 9.4%, UK 14.1%, Japan 17.6%,
China 23.8%, India 24.6%. No UAE/GCC-specific CAGR is published in the public summary.

Scope quoted from the report: plant care services covers "plant watering & feeding, pest/disease management,
repotting & soil services, landscaping & aesthetic care, subscription plant care kits."
Named players include **Ambius, Rentokil Initial Plants, Planterra, Natura, Greenscapes, The Sill, Bloomscape,
My City Plants** — i.e. FMI mixes B2B interiorscapers with D2C plant retailers, which inflates the number
and blurs the definition. **Methodology caution:** FMI does not publish a bottom-up build; historical series
is 2020–2024. Treat the absolute number as indicative only; the *segment shares* (on-site 65%, offices 40%)
are the more useful part.

### 1.2 IBISWorld "Office Plant Services in the US" — a number I could NOT verify

implausible.

### 1.3 Ambius / Rentokil Initial — the only hard corporate datapoint [CORP]

Ambius is the world's largest interior plantscaping brand and sits inside Rentokil Initial's
Hygiene & Wellbeing segment.

- FY2024: "Hygiene & Wellbeing Revenue increased by 8.4% (3.1% Organic) to £931m … Wellbeing grew Revenue by
  5.9% with softer performance in Dental offset by strength in Specialist Hygiene and **Ambius where we
  continue to see significant opportunity**." — Rentokil Initial 2024 Preliminary Results, 2025
  https://www.rentokil-initial.com/~/media/Files/R/Rentokil/documents/2024-preliminary-results.pdf
- FY2025: Hygiene & Wellbeing revenue **USD 1,205m, +4.3% (organic +2.3%)**; adjusted operating profit
  **USD 224m (FY24: 205m)**, i.e. **~18.6% adjusted operating margin**.
  https://www.rentokil-initial.com/~/media/Files/R/Rentokil/documents/2025-prelims-statement.pdf

**Why this matters for Planty:** Ambius is not separately disclosed, so the global interiorscape market is
*not* large enough for the category leader's parent to break it out. But the segment margin (~18% adjusted
operating) is a useful ceiling reference for a route-density services business at scale.
Note this segment also contains washroom hygiene, scenting, dental — plants are a minority of it.

### 1.5 Germany: the trade body exists, the numbers do not [ASSOC]

bottom-up.**

For scale reference on the German green-services adjacency, the **GaLaBau (landscape gardening) industry

https://www.handelsblatt.com/adv/presseportal/bundesverband-garten-landschafts-und-sportplatzbau-e.-v.-galabau---gruen-in-die-stadt-galabau-branchenstatistik-2025-mehr-umsatz-mehr-beschaeftigte-mehr-azubis-garten-und-landschaftsbau-behauptet-wachstumskurs-in-schwierigen-zeiten/100204076.html

## 2. The consumer houseplant market

### 2.1 Germany — actively contracting [ASSOC, high confidence]

Zentralverband Gartenbau (ZVG), reported 2026-01-26 [ASSOC]
https://www.derdeutschegartenbau.de/2026/01/26/blumen-und-zierpflanzen-anhaltende-kaufzurueckhaltung-belastet-den-markt/

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Total flowers & ornamental plants market (retail prices) | ~EUR 8.8bn (+~1%) | **EUR 8.5bn** |
| Per-capita spend | ~EUR 107 | **~EUR 102** (−EUR 2+, and EUR 6 below 2019) |
| **Zimmerpflanzen (indoor plants) market** | — | **EUR 1.4bn, −4.5% vs 2024** |
| Per-capita: flowering indoor plants | — | ~EUR 11 (−5%) |
| Per-capita: green indoor plants (Grünpflanzen) | — | **< EUR 6** (−<4%) |

Verbatim: *"kamen 1,4 Mrd. Euro als Marktvolumen zusammen, was einem Minus von 4,5 % zum Jahr 2024 entspricht"*
and *"sanken die Pro-Kopf-Ausgaben im vergangenen Jahr um mehr als 2 Euro auf rund 102 Euro"*.

2024 baseline for comparison (ZVG, 2025-01-27): market growth in 2024 was **purely price-driven**, not volume —
https://www.derdeutschegartenbau.de/2025/01/27/preisentwicklung-laesst-markt-wachsen/ and
https://lebensmittelpraxis.de/industrie-aktuell/42630-zentralverband-gartenbau-marktwachstum-war-2024-rein-preisgetrieben.html

**Blunt implication:** a German household spends **under EUR 6/year on green houseplants** (2025). A
B2C plant *rental* subscription at, say, EUR 25–40/month asks that household to increase its green-plant
spend by 50–80x. That is not a pricing problem, it is a category-creation problem.

### 2.4 Plant subscription boxes — real, small, and price-anchored low [ANEC/MR]

- Global "Plant Subscription Box Market" report exists but publishes no free headline figure —
  https://www.statsndata.org/report/plant-subscription-box-market-77363
- Price anchors (2025–2026, [ANEC]): **Horti from USD 20/month**; general plant subscription boxes
  **USD 20–65/month delivering 1–3 plants**.
  https://www.plantsolve.com/blog/plant-subscription-boxes-explained/
- Critique worth internalising: *"A $40/month box delivering 2 common Pothos cuttings in nursery pots is
  objectively poor value — those plants cost $8–$12 each at a grocery store."* (same source)
- Germany already has subscription incumbents: **Bloombox Club DE** (claims **200,000+ plants delivered to
  households and offices in Germany since 2015**, monthly/quarterly/6/12-month plans)
  https://bloomboxclub.de/ ; **PflanzMich Pflanzen-Abo** (5 young plants/month)
  https://www.pflanzmich.de/produkt/199497/pflanzen-abo.html ; **Plantbeat** https://plantbeat.shop/ ;
  **pflanzenabo.com** https://pflanzenabo.com/
- German B2B rental incumbent with published service cadence: **WELO Green "Mietpflanzen"** — Basic model for
  offices up to 200 m², **plant care every 3 weeks**, replacement guarantee.
  https://welo-green.de/pages/mietpflanzen

**Key structural point for Planty:** the D2C subscription anchor is **USD/EUR 20–65 per month for owned
plants**. Planty's rental price must sit near or below that band while also funding weekly technician visits
— which is arithmetically hard for B2C at low basket sizes. This is the central unit-economics tension of the
B2C side and shows up as a market-size constraint, not just a pricing one.

## 3. Product-as-a-service / rental economy as it applies to plants

- **Germany furniture rental market: USD 3,682.8m (2022) → USD 5,753.8m (2030), 5.7% CAGR** — Grand View
  Research Horizon [MR] https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/furniture-rental-market/germany
  (A separate FMI estimate puts Germany furniture rental at **8.7% CAGR 2025–2035** —
  https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/furniture-rental-market — again, direction agrees, level does not.)
- **Grover** (Berlin, founded 2015), the German PaaS reference case: **revenue surpassed EUR 100m in FY2022**;
  raised **USD 54m in July 2024**. https://tracxn.com/d/companies/grover/__G46FHg8yIzk-FiPVAqrigylQsLevHeL1FY4ceIzJ14o
  Grover's model — keep, switch, buy or return; refurbish and resell returns — is structurally the same
  economics Planty needs (asset utilisation across multiple rental cycles).
- Germany is structurally the *most* rental-native consumer market in the EU: **52.8% of the population lived
  in rented housing in 2025, the highest share in the EU** (France 38.6%, Spain 26.4%, Poland 12.8%) —
  Destatis [ASSOC]
  https://www.destatis.de/Europa/EN/Topic/Population-Labour-Social-Issues/Social-issues-living-conditions/RentedAccommodation.html
- Residential mobility: **~4.8m moves per year in Germany, ~10% of the population moving annually**
  ([ANEC]/secondary) https://x-stat.de/umzug-in-zahlen-wann-warum-und-wohin-deutschland-zieht/ ;
  renters move more often than owners (Statista, older survey data, 2011)
  https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/222598/umfrage/umzugshaeufigkeit-von-wohnungseigentuemer-und-mietern-im-vergleich/

**Read:** the "renters who move often" driver is genuinely strongest in Germany. But it cuts both ways —
a customer who moves every few years is a churn event *and* a logistics cost for Planty, not only a reason to
rent rather than buy.

## 5. Bottom-up sanity check (my own estimates — clearly labelled [EST])

These are **my arithmetic**, not sourced market figures. Shown so the vendor numbers can be pressure-tested.

**Berlin B2B [EST]:** 23.90m m² office stock (2025, sourced) × ~89.6% occupied (10.4% vacancy, sourced)
≈ 21.4m m² occupied. If interior plant rental were bought at a plausible EUR 0.30–0.60 per occupied m² per
year (my assumption — *unsourced*, derived from the c. EUR 20–40/plant/month price points other lenses
should confirm, at low plant density), Berlin's B2B interior-plant-rental market is on the order of
**EUR 6–13m/year**. Even at 3x that assumption it is a single-digit-to-low-tens EUR millions city market.
**Confidence: low. The per-m² assumption is the weak link and must be replaced with real competitor pricing.**

**Berlin B2C [EST]:** ~2.0m households; German green-houseplant spend <EUR 6/person/year (sourced, 2025).
Converting even 0.5% of Berlin households to a EUR 30/month rental = 10,000 × EUR 360 = **EUR 3.6m/year**,
but that requires a ~60x uplift in per-household green-plant spend. **Confidence: very low; treat as an
aspiration, not a TAM.**

**Dubai B2B [EST]:** UAE landscaping USD 1.67–1.8bn (sourced) × ~60% softscape (sourced) × Dubai ~36%
(sourced) ≈ USD 360–390m Dubai softscape. Interior plantscaping is plausibly 3–8% of softscape
(my assumption — *unsourced*) → **USD 11–31m/year Dubai interior plant services**. Comparable order of
magnitude to Berlin's B2B estimate but growing at ~9–10% vs a contracting German consumer backdrop and a
Berlin office market with 2.24m m² empty. **Confidence: low-moderate.**

## 6. Headwinds (do not skip these)

1. **German consumer plant spend is falling** (−4.5% indoor plants 2025; per-capita spend EUR 6 below 2019).
   ZVG attributes this to weakened purchasing power, real income losses and cost-of-living pressure.
2. **Berlin B2B demand is structurally soft** — 10.4% vacancy, take-up 40% below 10-year average,
   >11% vacancy forecast for 2026, and only 8% of German employees back in the office five days.
   Empty desks do not buy plants.
3. **D2C plant retail has already broken once** — The Sill's USD 6.7–6.9m annual losses and full retail
   closure (Oct 2024) are the cautionary case for a plant-logistics business with weekly touchpoints.
4. **The B2C rental category is unvalidated.** Every credible services number in this lens
   (FMI 39.8% commercial offices; Ambius; FvRH's historic "private down, business up") points to B2B being
   where the money is. I found **zero** market research validating consumer plant *rental* at scale.
5. **Data quality is poor.** Green wall estimates vary 10x; Mordor restated UAE FM by 2.6x; the IBISWorld
   "office plant services $28.1bn" figure is almost certainly mis-scoped. Any Planty deck quoting a single
   vendor number without a bottom-up cross-check will not survive diligence.
6. **UAE supply-side friction:** water scarcity, import logistics for exotic species, limited local
   cultivation capacity (MRFR, 2025). Plant sourcing cost/lead-time risk is higher in Dubai than in Germany,
   which sits next door to the Dutch auction system.
7. **AIPH's global read for 2024 is soft:** *"Many countries reported softening or stagnant demand compared
   with 2023, reflecting cautious consumer behaviour and ongoing cost pressures."*
   https://aiph.org/latest-news/crucial-global-data-released-international-statistics-flowers-and-plants-2025/
   (Global ornamental horticulture reported at ~USD 70bn in 2024 by secondary coverage —
   https://www.greenhousegrower.com/production/get-a-global-overview-of-the-ornamental-horticulture-market/ ;
   the underlying AIPH/Union Fleurs yearbook is paywalled at EUR 195 and compiled by University of Hohenheim,
   which is the credible primary source if Planty wants defensible global numbers.)
