# Planty — Customer research raw notes (personas, JTBD, willingness to pay)

Lens: Customer research. Compiled 2026-09-09. Every number carries a URL and the year of the figure. "Reported" = taken from the source; "Estimate" = my own inference, marked as such.

Method note: ~30 WebSearch queries (EN + DE + UAE-specific) and ~55 WebFetch page reads. Reddit (old/www), DuckDuckGo and Bing proxies were blocked or CAPTCHA'd from this environment, so first-person Reddit quotes could not be retrieved; forum evidence below comes from Trustpilot, Gulf News reader-facing pieces and competitor testimonials instead, and is labelled anecdotal. Google Trends could not be fetched (JS-only); no trend numbers are claimed.

---

## 1. Who actually pays for plant rental today (revealed demand)

### 1.1 Germany / Berlin — almost exclusively B2B, office-centric
- Plantclub (Berlin, founded 2020) says it serves "150+ Berlin teams" and names Figma, Netflix, GetYourGuide, Babbel, Taxfix, DEPT, Soho House, SoundCloud, Cushman & Wakefield, Moss as customers; care visits every 2 weeks; rating 5.0 (self-reported). https://plantclub.io/de/pflanzen-mieten-berlin (2025/26)
- Plantclub public pricing (2026): Greenhouse "from €200/month" up to 150 m²; Woodland "from €400/month" up to 500 m²; Jungle "from €600/month" up to 1,000 m²; Rainforest 1,000 m²+ custom. "Minimum term 12 months", "All prices excl. VAT", billed annually, delivery/installation and free replacements included. https://plantclub.io/de/preise
- Plantclub homepage: "Hundreds of teams" across Germany and Austria; named clients Contentful, GetYourGuide, Taxfix, Adina Hotel, N26, Occhio, Pliant, Cushman & Wakefield, Parship; "5.0 stars from 300+ reviews" (self-reported). Testimonial: "Guter und zuverlässiger Service – unsere Pflanzen sind hervorragend versorgt." https://plantclub.io/de (2026)
- Plant Circle for Business (Berlin): fixed monthly rate, design + maintenance included; clients HelloFresh, Mindspace (coworking), Otto Wulff, Eterno (medical/co-practice), Picsart, Bitpanda, Lightspeed, Industrious (coworking), Kerb Berlin. Segments listed: offices, coworking, healthcare waiting areas, cafés/restaurants. No pricing published. https://business.plantcircle.com/ (2026)
- 2bloom (Berlin): "keine Mindestlaufzeiten", care "im 2-Wochen-Intervall", hydroculture only; logos HelloFresh, Home24, Der Spiegel, Kayak, Riot Games. No public prices. https://www.2bloom.de/ (2026)
- WELO Green: "ab 199 €/Monat", 12-month minimum then monthly; page explicitly says buying can be "langfristig günstiger" for stable set-ups, renting suits "planbare Ausgaben, pflegefreie Begrünung". https://welo-green.de/blogs/journal/pflanzen-mieten-kosten (2025)
- Gärtner Gregg (Münster/Dortmund, traditional Hydrokultur trade): per-plant rent "Ab 6,90 €" (Ø 40 cm bowl) to "Ab 22,90 €"/month net for a 90×35×25 cm room divider; minimum term "In der Regel 12 Monate"; service "etwa alle vier Wochen"; free replacement. https://www.gaertner-gregg.de/pflanzenmiete-oder-leasing/ (2025)
- P2 Objekt Grün (Berlin, traditional): minimum merchandise value €2,000 net, minimum 5 plants, minimum term 36 months, rent computed from value of the installation. https://p2objektgruen.de/pflanzen-kaufen-mieten/ (2025)
- Rogel Raumbegrünung: "Ideal für Büro, Praxis & Empfang" — target segments named are office, medical practice, reception. https://hydrokultur.de/leistungen/mietpflanzen/ (2025)
- Berlin care-only providers (customer already owns plants): Büropflanzen Pflege Berlin — up to 30 medium plants €299 net/month, up to 50 plants €349 net/month, 2-week cadence, area Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf/Spandau. https://bueropflanzen-pflege.de/preise/ (prices "ab 2026"). Pflanzenpflege Berlin — €199 (≤30 plants), €249 (≤50), €299 (≤75) net/month, "2 Wochen Taktung", targets "Büros, Praxen und Gewerberäumen"; no private-household offer. https://pflanzenpflege-berlin.de/ (2026)
- Observation: none of the eight German providers examined publishes a private-household (B2C) rental tariff. B2C in Germany is served by purchase/subscription-delivery (e.g. easyplant self-watering pots) not by rental.

### 1.2 Dubai / UAE — B2B rental plus a real B2C *maintenance* market
- 800petals (Dubai): rental "quoted per plant per month, all-inclusive", "weekly maintenance visits by trained plant care staff", "free replacement of declining plants", seasonal rotation options, single monthly invoice; renting "suits most offices with 10+ plants"; "most clients choose 12-month agreements for the best monthly rate"; short-term for events/exhibitions/launches; serves DIFC, Business Bay, Media City, JLT, Downtown, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah. Their own rent-vs-buy FAQ: buying works best "over several years if paired with a maintenance contract". https://800petals.com/office-plant-rental-dubai/ (2026)
- Plantsworld.ae office rental page shows purchase prices AED 244–869 per office-size plant (Monstera, Areca, Rubber plant, Dracaena) but no rental tariff — "Talk to us" via WhatsApp. https://plantsworld.ae/collections/office-plant-rental-uae (2026)
- Plants Xpert: rental "Monthly, weekly, or event-based"; segments offices, villas ("Elevate the elegance of your home with premium indoor plants"), events, hotels. https://plantsxpert.com/pages/plants-rental-in-dubai-plants-xpert (2026)
- Desert Blooms: segments offices, hotels/restaurants, "villas, apartments, and retail stores", clinics, event spaces; stated reasons: "Eliminate the stress of plant care", "Buying premium indoor plants and decorative planters can be expensive". https://www.desertblooms.me/blogs/plant-care-guides/indoor-plant-rental-dubai-stylish-green-solutions-for-homes-offices-desertblooms (2025)
- Event hire: Table Art ("One-day corporate dinner, a week-long exhibition stand, or an ongoing office installation", no minimum), Hope Plants, Dubai Nursery, Plantscapes (rental "with or without the option of maintenance"). All quote-only. https://table-art.ae/plant-rentals/ ; https://hopeplantsdubai.com/indoor-plant-hire ; https://www.dubainursery.ae/plant-rentals/ ; https://plantscapes.ae/plant-rentals/ (2026)
- B2C maintenance price points (customer owns the plants) — this is the clearest revealed WTP signal in either market:
  - Royal Plantscape: 1 visit/month AED 120; 2 visits/month AED 299; weekly AED 499/month; twice-weekly AED 799/month; à-la-carte "from AED 199/visit"; green-wall maintenance AED 499/month minimum; segments villas, apartments, balconies, offices, coworking, hotels, clinics. https://www.royalplantscape.com/pages/indoor-plant-maintenance-service (2026)
  - Plants Xpert: "Starting from just AED 149/month", weekly or bi-weekly visits, "FREE Plant Replacement (T&Cs apply)", "7-Day Green Guarantee"; copy says "most plants die within weeks" due to "poor maintenance" and "light, humidity, and AC exposure"; addresses "homeowners and office managers". https://plantsxpert.com/blogs/news/monthly-indoor-plant-maintenance-package (2026)
  - Greenly.ae (app-based, on-demand): Plant Care from AED 179/visit, Plant Sitting from AED 99, Watering from AED 99, Pest control AED 199; recurring bookings "charged on a monthly basis until you cancel"; targets apartments, villas, businesses, "vacation travelers"; "The extreme heat, intense sun, and humidity in Dubai make taking care of plants a challenge." https://greenly.ae/services-and-pricing/plant-maintenance-services-dubai/ (2026)
  - Plantshop.me sells a "Monthly Indoor Plant Maintenance Package" (weekly visit: pruning, dusting, watering, misting, fertilising, pest treatment) — page blocked (403), price not read. https://www.plantshop.me/ae-en/product/monthly-indoor-plant-maintenance-package
  - Search-result summary reported packages "starting from AED 49" from some providers (not verified on-page).
- 800petals' own retail arm targets homes only with purchases (iwantflower.com); no B2C rental. https://800petals.com/ (2026)

Takeaway (my inference): In Dubai a paid, recurring, technician-visits-your-home plant-care habit already exists at AED 120–500/month; in Berlin it does not exist as a published product. That is the single biggest customer-side difference between the two markets.

## 2. Personas, jobs-to-be-done, triggers, objections, WTP

### 2.1 B2B — Office manager / People & Culture / Facilities at SMEs and startups (PRIMARY)
- Who decides: In startups the office manager "is often one of the first employees hired" and handles far more than the physical office (Gusto job-description guide, via search summary). Berlin Startup Jobs' office-management category currently lists a Datawrapper "Office-Manager:in" role whose text stresses "wie wichtig eine angenehme und produktive Arbeitsumgebung ist" (2026). https://berlinstartupjobs.com/skill-areas/office-management/ Anecdotal.
- Plantclub's client roster (Figma, N26, Taxfix, GetYourGuide, SoundCloud, Babbel) confirms the buyer is the tech-startup/scale-up office team; Plantclub also sells "team workshops" and "office moves as you grow" as plan features, i.e. it is positioned as an employee-experience/perk purchase, not a facilities purchase. https://plantclub.io/de/preise (2026)
- JTBD: "Make the office look cared-for and alive for employees and visitors without me (or a rota of volunteers) being responsible when plants die." Oxygen at Work frames the failure modes of DIY: pests spreading unnoticed, "missed watering during holidays", knowledge loss with staff turnover; and the rental value as "one transparent annual fee" and adaptation to "hybrid work patterns, and office relocations". https://www.oxygenatwork.com/en/post/renting-or-buying-office-plants (2025)
- Triggers: new office / move / expansion (Plantclub: "Free office moves as you grow"); return-to-office and hybrid re-design; wellbeing programmes; WELL certification.
- Evidence they can use internally (ROI story):
  - Human Spaces (Interface/Cary Cooper, 2015): 7,600 office workers, 16 countries incl. Germany and UAE; 58% have no live plants in workspace, 47% no natural light; natural elements associated with +15% wellbeing, +6% productivity, +15% creativity; 33% say office design would affect their decision to work somewhere. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/global-study-connects-levels-of-employee-productivity-and-well-being-to-office-design-300058034.html (2015; report PDF https://www.interface.com/content/dam/interfaceinc/interface/global-campaigns/human-spaces/report/global-human-spaces-report/Human%20Spaces%20report%202015%20EN.pdf)
  - Exeter/Groningen/Queensland field experiments (Nieuwenhuis, Knight, Postmes, Haslam, J. Exp. Psych.: Applied, 2014): enriching a "lean" office with plants raised productivity ~15% in two large commercial offices (UK, NL); also higher workplace satisfaction, concentration, perceived air quality. https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2014/09/140901090735.htm (2014)
  - UTS Sydney (2010, cited by WeWork): plants reduced anxiety 37%, depression 58%, anger/hostility 44%, chronic fatigue 40% — small study, treat as directional. https://www.wework.com/ideas/research-insights/biophilic-five-benefits-of-office-plants
  - Terrapin "Economics of Biophilia" (2015): "productivity costs are 112 times greater than energy costs in the workplace"; views to nature "can save over $2,000 per employee per year". https://www.terrapinbrightgreen.com/report/economics-of-biophilia/
  - Real-estate angle (RealVal via Plant Circle blog, 2025): biophilic offices "5–10% rental premiums", "16% lower tenant turnover", leasing cycles "approximately 3.5 weeks shorter"; Berlin office vacancy 7.5% and take-up down 16% YoY (CBRE Q3 2025) — landlords/coworking operators are competing on amenities. https://business.plantcircle.com/blog/why-premium-green-design-is-a-strategic-advantage-in-berlins-office-market
  - WELL v2 Mind concept: M02 Access to Nature accepts indoor plants (potted, plant beds or living walls) as a compliance path; M09 Enhanced Access to Nature adds quantitative thresholds (search summary via Stok/Exubia; WELL pages fetched did not expose the numeric thresholds, so the "1% of floor area / 2% living wall" figures are second-hand). https://stok.com/news-insights/biophilic-design-sneaking-into-standards/ ; https://www.exubia.co.uk/biophilia-for-sustainability-and-well-being-certifications/ (2024–25)
- Objections (as surfaced by providers' own FAQs/blogs): (1) "buying looks cheaper on paper" — Oxygen at Work; WELO concedes buying is cheaper long-term for a stable set-up; (2) 12-month lock-in is the industry norm (Plantclub, WELO, Gärtner Gregg, 800petals), 36 months at P2 Objekt Grün; only 2bloom advertises "keine Mindestlaufzeiten" — a wedge; (3) access/keys/visit scheduling (Desert Blooms hospitality copy: "work around operational hours to ensure zero disruption").
- Willingness to pay (reported list prices): Berlin design-led providers €200–600/month for 150–1,000 m² (Plantclub), i.e. roughly €1.3–0.6 per m² per month (my arithmetic); traditional trade €7–23 per plant per month net; care-only €199–349/month for 30–75 plants (≈ €4.5–7 per plant per month, my arithmetic). Dubai: no public per-plant rental tariff found; maintenance-only AED 120–799/month by visit frequency (Royal Plantscape 2026).

### 2.2 B2B — Coworking operators
- Berlin: Mindspace and Industrious are named Plant Circle clients (2026). Architizer describes coworking spaces with "over 2,000 individual plants and trees from 100 different species, maintained by professional gardening teams" (via search summary, 2024). https://business.plantcircle.com/ ; https://architizer.com/blog/inspiration/collections/biophilic-design-coworking-spaces/
- JTBD: "Differentiate the space in a soft office market and keep members longer"; buyer = community/operations manager; lock-in acceptable because the asset is the space itself; cadence weekly because of visitor churn. Coworking operators face the same 7.5% vacancy pressure (CBRE Q3 2025 via Plant Circle).

### 2.3 B2B — Hotels, restaurants, clinics, showrooms
- Dubai providers list hotels/restaurants/clinics/showrooms explicitly (Desert Blooms, Adplants "office, hotel, showroom, or corporate event", Royal Plantscape "hospitals and clinics"). Berlin: Adina Hotel (Plantclub), Eterno health spaces and cafés (Plant Circle), "Praxis & Empfang" (Rogel). https://adplants.com/pages/plant-rentals ; https://plantclub.io/de
- JTBD: first impressions ("lush, healthy plants signal quality and attention to detail" — Desert Blooms hospitality copy); decision maker = GM / F&B or practice owner; visits must be outside opening hours. Higher WTP for large statement specimens (Plantsworld sells office-size plants at AED 244–869 each, 2026).

### 2.4 B2B — Real-estate staging and events
- Dubai staging guides list "a lot of plants" among the small details crucial to appeal (homeworlddesign, 2025) and Indigo Living sells staging packages; plant-rental firms (Plants Xpert, Desert Blooms) list villas and events. https://homeworlddesign.com/home-staging-in-dubai-trends-and-tips-for-property-owners/ ; https://indigo-living.ae/pages/homestaging
- Events are the only segment where short durations (1 day – 1 month) are standard and where "no minimum order" is advertised (Table Art). Fit with Planty's "2 weeks / 1 month" durations is best here, but it is a logistics business (overnight set-up, collection), not a subscription.

### 2.5 B2C — "I kill every plant" / time-poor plant lover
- Over 50% of UK houseplant owners have killed an average of 5 plants; overwatering blamed by 54%, insufficient light 30%; 48% of 25–39-year-olds worry about keeping houseplants alive and 47% of that age group who don't own plants cite care concerns; 81% of millennials say houseplants positively impact mental health. https://horticulture.co.uk/houseplants/statistics/ (compiled 2024, underlying surveys 2020–2022)
- Article/OnePoll (US, 2020): 70% of millennials identify as "plant parents", average plant parent has killed 7 houseplants, 67% call themselves "plant murderers", 48% "troubled by plant care abilities". https://gardenpals.com/houseplant-statistics/ (2024 compilation of 2020 data)
- Global indoor-plant market ~USD 21 bn in 2025, 4–5% CAGR to 2030; online channel growing ~10% CAGR (Mordor); search interest stabilised above pre-pandemic. https://terrariumtribe.com/houseplant-statistics/ (2026)
- Revealed substitute: easyplant (self-watering pots, purchase) has 4.8/5 from 5,709 Trustpilot reviews; typical buyer quote: "I always end up either over watering or underwater...the self-watering pots" (Robert, GB); complaint: "Very expensive". https://www.trustpilot.com/review/easyplant.com (2026, anecdotal)
- JTBD: "Have a green home that looks like a plant person lives here, without becoming one." Trigger: moving in, a dead plant, a gift/event. Objections: price vs IKEA/garden centre, a stranger weekly, contract. WTP: no survey found; only proxies — UK average spend £306.71/person/year on plants (2021, horticulture.co.uk) ≈ £25/month, which is the ceiling a mainstream consumer already spends on plants; Dubai maintenance buyers pay AED 120–499/month (2026).

### 2.6 B2C — Expats / renters who move often or travel long
- Dubai: 92% of 3.95 m residents are expatriates (June 2025), 69% aged 25–54. https://www.globalmediainsight.com/blog/dubai-population-statistics/ (2025)
- Tenure is lengthening, which weakens the "I'm only here 2 years" rental argument: Betterhomes Future Living Report 2025 — average residency 10.5 years (7.5 in 2024); renters 9.9 years and 59% plan to stay 10+ years. https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-residents-put-down-roots-as-average-tenure-surges-to-over-a-decade (2025)
- But summer absence is a strong, seasonal trigger: Gulf News reports residents "typically leave for two to three weeks during summer vacation" and unattended plants "wilt within days and die"; Dubai Garden Centre ran a free "Plant Summer Camp" (max 3 indoor plants, ≤1.25 m) with strong uptake including from Abu Dhabi (2018). https://gulfnews.com/xpress/news/green-summer-how-to-keep-your-garden-green-when-you-are-away-1.2253536 ; https://gulfnews.com/uae/free-service-tends-to-your-plants-1.1558312 (2018) — anecdotal/dated.
- Climate/AC pain: "Switching the AC on and off may help you cut down on electricity bills, but it will shorten the life of your plants." — Yamini Ved, MD Environ, Gulf News 2019; ideal 20–25 °C constant. Overwatering is "the number one plant killer" (Harly Sabater, Abu Dhabi expat, Gulf News 2022). https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/uae%2Fcreating-an-indoor-oasis-1.433656 ; https://gulfnews.com/friday/home/your-starter-pack-to-keeping-your-house-plants-alive-from-a-dedicated-plant-dad-1.1643275126240
- Berlin: no comparable "who waters my plants" service tariff found; providers are B2B only (see 1.1). Renters/expats in Berlin are a plausible persona but no evidence of paid behaviour was found.

### 2.7 B2C — Families with pets / small children (toxicity)
- ASPCA Animal Poison Control: >451,000 cases in 2024; plants & fungi were the 6th-largest category at 8.1% of exposures (≈36,500 cases, my arithmetic); ~66% dogs / 34% cats of plant calls; >700 plant species listed toxic, incl. common houseplants lilies, sago palm, pothos. https://www.aspca.org/news/official-top-10-toxins-2024 (2024)
- JTBD: "Green home that is certified pet-safe/child-safe", a curation job Planty can own via a "pet-safe" catalogue filter. No WTP data found; treat as a differentiation feature, not a segment.

### 2.8 B2C — Wealthy villa owners (Dubai)
- Every Dubai provider lists villas; Royal Plantscape's weekly (AED 499) and twice-weekly (AED 799) tiers and "green wall maintenance AED 499/month minimum" (2026) show villa-scale recurring spend. Plants Xpert markets "premium indoor plants" for villas. Decision maker is the homeowner or household manager; access via domestic staff is normal, which lowers the "stranger in my home" objection relative to Berlin. https://www.royalplantscape.com/pages/indoor-plant-maintenance-service
- Garden-maintenance-cost guides exist for Dubai villas (floweryduae.com, 2026) — outdoor landscaping contracts are a common upsell path.

## 4. Gaps and caveats
- No direct consumer willingness-to-pay survey for plant rental in either market was found; WTP is inferred from published maintenance tariffs (Dubai) and plant spend (UK proxy).
- Reddit threads (r/houseplants, r/dubai, r/berlin, r/officemanagers) were not retrievable from this environment; first-person quotes are from Trustpilot and Gulf News only.
- Google Trends not retrieved.
- Human Spaces per-country (Germany/UAE) breakdowns are in the PDF but were not extractable; global figures used.
- WELL numeric thresholds are second-hand.
- Dubai per-plant rental prices are quote-only everywhere; only maintenance tariffs are public.
